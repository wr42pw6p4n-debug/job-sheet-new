⚠ Get this into Safari first

On iPad, "Open in Safari" isn't in the Files long-press menu

1. Files app → find this file → tap once to preview it (Quick Look opens)
2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow, top-right of Quick Look) — not a long-press
3. In the share sheet, scroll the row of app icons and tap Safari
4. Once it's open in Safari, tap Aa in the address bar → Add to Home Screen — it'll then open full-screen like a real app, ready for the crane yard, no re-downloading needed.

Saved Reports

Reports you save are stored in this browser on this device — they won't appear if you open the file on a different phone or computer, or in private/incognito mode.

Equipment History

Every visit to a piece of equipment, newest first — what was found and what work was carried out. Combine reports from other phones

With OneDrive sync switched on (top of the page) reports arrive on their own. Otherwise, they're saved on the phone they were written on: on each engineer's phone, open this section, pick the week and tap Export, then send the file over. On this device, tap Add reports and choose the file(s) — the full reports are merged in (they'll show up in Saved Reports too, not just the list below), minus photos and signatures to keep the file small.
